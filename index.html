I am completing my PhD in the Department of Mathematics at UC Berkeley. I am broadly interested in probability theory, machine learning, and their applications to biology. I am advised by Steven Evans.

My research includes Doob–Martin boundaries of uniformly generated d-ary trees and extensions of graphon theory to combinatorial stochastic processes with related exchangeability structures.

Before Berkeley, I studied mathematics and applied computational mathematical sciences at the University of Washington, where I worked with Eric Shea-Brown.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy biking through San Francisco, reading nonfiction, and climbing at the many excellent crags around the Bay Area.
